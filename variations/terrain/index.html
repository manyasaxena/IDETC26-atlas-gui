About this concept

Terrain

The field is drawn as land: concepts that are often studied in the same papers sit close together on the ground, and the land rises wherever many papers fall, so mountains are heavily studied areas and valleys are thin ones. Change the years and the land reshapes; click a region or a pin to fly down to a concept, its connections and the papers behind them.

Ground positions come from a seeded force layout over co-occurrence strength (shared papers ÷ √(papers A × papers B)), so distances are approximate. Height is a smoothed sum of each visible concept's paper count, square-root-ish compressed and scaled to the full 1989–2025 total, so growing years can be compared. If a mountain hides something, switch to Map (M).
