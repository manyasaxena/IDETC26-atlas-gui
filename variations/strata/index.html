About Strata

The Atlas is drawn as four stacked layers that read like the anatomy of a study: who is studied, what they do, what they work on, and what is measured. You explore by drilling a core sample down through the layers, and the sample holds the real papers that cover every concept you picked.

An empty sample is not an error: it may be a gap in the literature worth studying.
