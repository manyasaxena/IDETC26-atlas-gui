About this concept: a watershed in time

The Atlas is drawn as a watershed: every concept is a stream that rises in the year it was first studied and widens with the papers published on it each year, flowing left to right from 1989 to 2025 in four channels (subjects, tasks, problems, constructs). Where two streams share papers you see a confluence, a dot at the year those topics met in the same study.

It is flat on purpose, a counterpoint to the 3D concepts: nothing hides behind anything and every label is readable; depth comes from opening and closing channels.
