About this concept

The field is drawn as a galaxy: each star system is a neighborhood of concepts that tend to be studied in the same papers, and systems that share more papers sit closer together. You travel inward, from galaxy to system to a single concept, then to one connection and the papers behind it.

It is 2.5D on purpose: tilt, depth ordering and parallax give a sense of space, while every label stays flat and readable. Esc or Back moves you out one level.
