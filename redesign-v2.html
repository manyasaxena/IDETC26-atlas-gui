What’s in the Atlas

Source
Papers from the ASME IDETC Design Theory and Methodology (DTM) conference, 1989–2025. The 1992 and 2016 proceedings aren’t included.
Scope
601 empirical papers out of 1,548: studies where someone performs a design task that’s observed or measured.
Not included (yet)
Journals like the Journal of Mechanical Design or Design Science, and non-empirical papers. This is a first step, described in the IDETC 2026 paper.
How it was built
Concepts were extracted by an AI pipeline, and paper summaries are AI-written from abstracts, so check important details in the original paper.
